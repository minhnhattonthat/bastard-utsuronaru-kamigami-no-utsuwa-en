# Synopsis

Built as the script is translated: each batch adds what it covers. Guides were not found; the script decides.

## Premise
Dark Schneider (D.S.) wakes on a beach with no memory but his own name. An orb holding the soul of Tia Noto Yoko falls into his hands and heals him; a voice begged him to wake. He sets out to find her. Everyone he meets has lost their memory too.

## Chapter 1
- **Forgotten Beach (`s0010`):** D.S. finds Vai, a young swordsman, sitting by a dying fire, drained of will. The orb revives him: he too remembers only his name and Yoko. They bicker over her and go on together. A dragon-prowed ship lies grounded offshore.
- **Dragon Ship (`s0014`):** brand-new, crewed by dragontooth soldiers. Joshua (samurai, knows more than he says, comes and goes on "a mission of his own") tells them comrades here were all sent to help D.S. and "the true enemy" is ahead of them. Blad (pompous giant knight) wakes standing like a statue; Bol (shadow user, `ござる` speech, terrified of something unseen) fights them mad with fear, then joins. A blue healing sphere from Yoko restores them (these recur).
- **Earth Dragon Temple (`s0018`, `s0022`):** a monolith speaks only to D.S.: "thy power relies not on magic alone". The keystone reflects his Damned back. Broken by teamwork, it speaks: the Earth Dragon, "already defiled", sealed the land, and asks to be freed so they can "open the spiral road". Footplate doors need three people.
- **Gorge (`s0030`):** Macalpine (demon binder, right eye hidden: it's a flash device) and Jorg (Joshua's sworn friend, no memory, "I have no friends") ambush them for the High Ninja, who has a huge fort and an army. D.S.'s torture bluff; Macalpine escapes, D.S. lets Jorg go, Joshua chases him. Yngwei (calm swordsman, no memory) joins at the altar; its dragon horn opens the cave.
- **Cave (`s0034`):** dimensional "hole": D.S. or Vai pulls out either an arm accessory (booster, later Sykes's) or a cursed harp (later Shella's lyre).
- **Great gorge (`s0038`):** the walking fort chases Princess Sheila. The High Ninja (giant in an ogre mask) floors Blad with one punch. Sheila gives herself up to save them, and silently asks D.S. to save her later; she sets off the spirit reactor, so he must retreat. Yngwei runs after her (he once swore to serve someone like her). Sykes steps out of a dimensional window: this is an isolated world, not theirs; Sheila is the catalyst for a reactor meant to open the World-Breaker Gate, so the fort can leave for another dimension; great magical beings stand behind the High Ninja. A hound hunts Sykes: with the booster he seals it (or, without it, runs). Blad goes off alone. The ninjas are remodeled goblins. The Ogre Wail Gate shuts. Zakk (teen brawler, fooled, thinks they are bandits) guards it, can't open it, and fights on every visit.
- **Lake (`s0050`):** beyond a cave fork, the fort stands on an island in a man-made caldera lake full of giant fish. Shella (bard, druidess blood, hears the Earth Dragon: sewn down by thunder and steel) gets her lyre back and joins or leaves. Ba Thory (bug tamer, regenerates, bad fishing ambush) joins, then gets swallowed by a fish.
- **Wind God Tower (`s0058`, `s0068`):** wind power feeds the Thunder God Tower across the valley. Ros (lockpick swordsman, camp speech, insists he's no queen, hunting "something") loots it first. The Wind Machine God is guardian and power control.
- **Power line (`s0064`):** Macalpine and Jorg ambush them on the cable. Joshua takes D.S.'s fake Damned for Jorg, whose memory returns: captain of a hundred samurai, Joshua's sworn friend. Jorg joins in Joshua's place; Joshua leaves again. Macalpine flees.
- **Thunder God Tower (`s0072`):** Zion and his wounded brother Schen, lost and hunted by monsters.
- **Tower depths (`s0072`-`s0080`):** Zion and Schen join for a while (Schen half-remembers a wife, and fears remembering; Ba Thory gives him near-death flashes). A ghost possesses the orb to show a transparent bridge. Below, the ghost is Jin Simon, a ninja who lived inside a sword and lost it; he shares the orb with Yoko, to D.S.'s rage. Angus (giant, near silent, beeps, "like a machine") materializes in a burial chamber. The sword is the Murasame, sealing the Earth Dragon alongside the electric field. Jin returns to it, D.S. draws it, and the dragon, "the one power", goes berserk; its foes are "the ancient, hollow ones who covet power".
- **Sykes's theory (`s0076`):** no native humans here; everyone human has ties to each other from before. Was the High Ninja one too?
- **Rock caves (`s0084`-`s0108`):** six crystals in the hexagonal rock boost the spirit reactor. D.S. binds each with his seal for one big spell. A death-illusion pillar shows Jin's memory. Bol's shadow demon is his own guilt: a runaway from a shadow clan, forced to kill his friends; D.S. punches him back to life.
- **Fort (`s0114`, `s0118`):** Sheila is freed from the spirit reactor. Yngwei: she's not his mistress after all. The High Ninja's mask breaks: he is Gara, Ninja Master, D.S.'s old friend. Jin in the Murasame drains him; the parasite in his ear with a shadow becomes the Slaughter Emperor, a fallen god who hoped to regain rank in "that dimension" ("those others" would jeer; "was I being played?"). D.S. fires the Seven-Key Guardian through his crystals, destroying the god, the Gate and the fort. Backstory: Jin and Gara trained together; Jin won the Murasame but envied Gara and died using the Omen Ninja Sword on him. The empty Dragon Ship takes them along a hidden waterway. Gara leaves to train; Yngwei leaves too.

## Chapter 2 (forest)
- **Sea of trees (`s0122`-`s0146`):** the ship grounds in giant roots. The Dragon Tree towers at the center of the forest. The Demon Thunder Queen (half dark elf, witch of the Tree-Coffin Castle, three phantom bodies of the Three Fiends, e.g. King Raven) rules by fear. Kai Harn (woman swordsman, says `俺`) and Sean (`わたし`) are of the Kido Corps, war orphans raised with Ran; Ran (brother of Sean) serves the queen as Black Knight, brainwashed. Their lord resembles the queen: Kai must learn if she is the true lord. Di is also missing. Mistletoe Dagger undoes oak-root seals. Lake demon Teutates; Schen is in an oak prison. Inside the Dragon Tree, a wyvern's corpse hangs in vines, heart taken, held by Esus. (Batches jump back and forth here; order uncertain.)
- **Wyvern's heart (`s0146`):** Esus drops the Wyvern's Left Eye and flees; "my big brother's curse" lies on the Dragon Tree. Sykes: the dragon lives on through its gouged-out heart, too strong for the demons to carry. Light through the wound marks the spot. Only D.S. is in sync with the heart: it amplifies him and wakes buried knowledge (Sykes: "what is a dragon?"). Taranis (second brother, lightning) sets the wyvern and tree ablaze; the party flees.

- **The wyvern reborn (`s0146`):** burning, it rises like a phoenix, and spirals north through the Wall of Thorns (same "spiral road" as the Earth Dragon).
- **Forest, earlier stretch (`s0153`-`s0191`):** Shella hears the forest and sings of a captive dragon. Schen is spirited away by a half-elf girl of 14 or so (dark elf blood), a phantom of the queen, who says Schen looks for a forgotten wife, "not me"; freeing him with the Mistletoe Dagger costs Zion, sucked into a dimensional rift in Schen's place (or both vanish). Poison Maiden fights them, and flees. Horsehead statues beam a mother's voice into Vai, who attacks. Ran, Black Knight, "mother forest". The last phantom (24 or so) holds Ran as a son; the armor crushes him when he refuses; Horsehead Mother fights; Macalpine helps, then joins or leaves (Undine kiss-mark trick). Ran is freed and joins; he and Kai recall the Kido Corps; Di is missing.
- **Phantom Castle (`s0163`, `s0170`):** drying the Upper Phantom Lake bares a castle. Ba Thory returns or joins with a dried bug; Ros bets D.S. for a synapse cable. Di-Amon (vampire, drained by the queen's ritual, now a frail, pretty boy who thinks himself hideous and his macho form beautiful) polishes floors. An earring from a dead bat holds his magic: he turns into a tattooed musclebound giant; D.S. either bluffs a decay spell into it or lays the Blue Nail Evil Core Curse. Teutates, water demon, eldest of the Three Male Gods, drops a key.
- **Dead woods and hedge maze (`s0174`, `s0181`):** King Raven (armored half-elf phantom with a raven) fights them; the half-elf falters whenever D.S. looks at her. Joshua returns (Kai's name comes back to him and he flirts; Ran bristles). A mural's left eye takes the Wyvern's Left Eye; its split spirit clears the sky over the Dragon Tree.
- **Temple of Dite (`s0187`):** Ida Deesna, painted giant, summoner, "slave of beautiful things", serves a goddess of death; joins after a beautiful fight. D.S. wants to seal her and two more demon gods in his body.
- **Lake (`s0191`):** Undines carry the party to an island if one man stays (comedy per choice; Macalpine gets dumped on them). Sean Ari, talisman sorceress, is freed from the sacrifice pillars; a giant crayfish dies and blocks the river, draining the other lake.
- **Tree-Coffin Castle (`s0195`):** the queen in the flesh, turned vampire by Di's magic, voiced by the Three Fiends. A little girl phantom asks "Who are you?": she is the queen at 6 or 7; "Nei" = "no". The queen flees to the western spire. The Dream Mirror projects a boy with a machete, young Jorg; he walks through the pain and forgives himself. Jorg's backstory: son of a disgraced Kwa Po master, enslaved by bandits until twelve; a samurai force captures them.

- **Jorg's secret (`s0195`):** Nils Shawn Mifune, top samurai and his father's friend, saves Jorg from execution. Joshua, "like the sun", breaks his hatred. The chest holds his bandit machete: he killed the chief from behind and never told Joshua. He beats the Dream Mirror's boy phantom by facing it.
- **End of the forest (`s0199`):** Macalpine loses his duel and joins or stays. D.S. wrings the "wind" magic out of the Three Male Gods into a crystal. Yngwei: his lord is a man, "ice". The queen, rebrainwashed, is purged of vampire magic by that wind crystal. Vision: D.S. named a half-elf orphan Arshes Nei; she lived with him a hundred years as his daughter (calls him Dahsh, insists she's his lover). The Three Fiends merge as Trivun; D.S. and Nei's Heaven-Earth Blast (Di Vumu Stein) destroys her and the castle. Nei raised Di and the Kido Corps. Shadows of Loki and Octol talk: "that one" takes his time; Manipura reached too early; Loki's domain is next, then Octol's.

## Chapter 3 (snowfield)
- **Snowfield (`s0203`, `s0207`):** poisonous aurora; Ice Hell Tower on an ice bridge between twin peaks. Masked High King = Kall-Su, D.S.'s lackey, calls his Sorcerer Generals: Yngwei, Ran, Zakk, Ros, Blad, Bol, Ba Thory, Ida, Sykes, Shella, Macalpine remember. Most go; Shella, Sykes, Ida, Macalpine may stay. Kai and/or Sean chase Ran. Nei scouts alone: Kall has shown his name, so no name trick can free him. Zion and Schen's shadow: "a black figure". Great beast swims under the snow.
- **Ba Thory, third try:** gets his strange bug back and joins, or D.S. kicks him in as bait.
- **Mountains (`s0223`):** electromagnetic storm from the aurora blocks the climb; Sykes warns of it. Angus glitches ("Radio Ga Ga"); Sykes suspects who he is.
- **Water Dragon (`s0230`, `s0215`):** frozen solid under the tower by Kall's ice; lifts the water barrier on the Frozen Lake Maze. Ether spring (Vai dunked on a rope) shows ruins under a sea of ether. Demon Ruby lures the great beast.
- **Inside the beast (`s0234`):** Bol attacks on orders; Kall's "healing device" is a bomb; D.S. takes it with his arm (regrows). Bol joins. Zion and Schen return from the dark hole; Zion has memories, a crooked smile, gave Schen the Black Yaksha. Shella recalls a Sorcerer General "demon swordsman" Zion. Vai's tickling sneezes them out.
- **Ruins (`s0238`, `s0256`, `s0260`):** five palaces plus a tower, star layout, rearranged out of true; the party shifts them back (zodiac) to clear the aurora. Ran fights to the death; Sean or Kai breaks through; Joshua comes along.

## Chapter 1, side note
- **Dragon's release (`s0042`, `s0054`):** the freed Earth Dragon spirals down the gorge, smashes the Ogre Wail Gate and drains the lake. If they go back for Zakk, D.S. makes Macalpine haul all of them up the cliff on his wire; Zakk then joins or leaves, and Macalpine and Ros leave. The Hermit Rock Fort stands revealed; D.S. feels power surging since the dragon flew off.
